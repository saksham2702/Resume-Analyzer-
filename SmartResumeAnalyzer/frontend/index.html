About CareerFit

CareerFit is a platform designed to help users understand their resumes and explore job opportunities based on their skills and qualifications.

Users can write or paste their resumes for analysis, review feedback, and compare their profiles against job descriptions they provide.

Our goal is to make resume evaluation and job matching easier to understand through a simple and accessible platform.

01

Resume Analysis

Write or paste a resume, review information returned by the analysis service, and explore professional feedback.

02

Job Matching

Compare resume information with a job description you provide and review the matching results available to you.

03

Skill Insights

Explore skills identified in a resume and see which job requirements may not appear in the comparison.

The details

A few things to know.

Clear answers, and honest notes where the details depend on the current implementation.

What is CareerFit?

CareerFit is designed to help you understand your resume and explore how your skills may align with job opportunities.

How does Resume Analysis work?

Write or paste your resume text and request analysis. Results depend on the analysis service connected to the application.

How do I add my resume?

Write or paste your resume as plain text in the Resume Analyzer. File uploads are not part of this workflow.

Can I analyze more than one resume?

This has not been confirmed in the current project files. The number of resumes you can analyze may depend on the implementation available to you.

How does Job Matching work?

The intended workflow compares information from a resume with job requirements and shows matching results. The exact comparison depends on the current implementation.

Do I need to provide my own job description?

The described matching workflow uses a job description you provide. Please check the current application for the options available in your version.

Does CareerFit guarantee a job?

No. Resume feedback and matching results are informational and cannot guarantee an interview, offer, or job.

Is my resume information stored?

Resume text is sent only to the connected backend when you explicitly save or analyze it. The current frontend does not keep resume text in browser storage.
